Machine Embroidery (2022)

Chain

Description

The CNC embroidery machines in the makerspace I volunteer in are some of my favorite machines. The pi cat was embroidered into my shop apron and was done with Embriliance software. The same goes for the beaver logo. Both designs were embroidered using Brother SE400 sewing/embroidery machines.

Lessons Learned

I have gained a lot more respect for people who do work with textiles. I probably broke 4 needles in the learning process. I also learned to use backing on fuzzy fabrics.
